# Enjeu: Terrain

**An expansion module.** The base game is complete without it. Leave these cards
in the box and nothing else changes.

Everything here is additive: no card in the 111 changes, and no rule in
[RULES.md](RULES.md) is replaced. If you have already printed your deck, this
module costs you two sheets and nothing else.

**What it adds:** the ground fights too. Places can hurt you, and you can use
them on the boss.

---

## 1. The Mark

> A Mark is a brick you put under a figure. It does one thing. It goes away.

That is the whole idea. The brick is the rule: if there is no brick under a
figure, nothing is happening to it.

**The five rules:**

1. A Mark sits under the figure it affects, where everyone can see it.
2. A Mark does its thing at the **start of a round**, all at once, for everybody.
3. A Mark ends when its card says. Most end by themselves.
4. **The same Mark twice does not stack.** The second one just refreshes the first.
5. Every Mark is small. One that ticks costs 25 a round, one that shifts a
   check moves it one step, and **Charged is the single exception**: +50, once,
   and its card says so. You will never add two Marks together.

Rules 4 and 5 are why this stays a game and does not become homework. You will
never add two Marks together, and you will never hold a number that is not on
the Damage Track.

### The six Marks

| Mark | Brick | What it does | How it ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poison** | green | Lose 25 at the start of each round | Spend an action to shake it off |
| **Burning** | red | Lose 25 at the start of the round, then it goes out | By itself. Hide first and it goes out instead |
| **Frozen** | light blue | Lose your next action. A boss loses its whole turn | By itself, after one turn |
| **Marked** | white | Attacks against this figure are one step easier | When an attack lands |
| **Charged** | yellow | Your next landed attack deals +50 | As soon as a hit costs you a card |
| **Snared** | brown | You cannot Run and cannot Hide. A boss cannot Brace | Spend an action to break free |

Any brick of roughly the right colour will do. If you have no light blue, use
whatever your table agrees means cold, and say it out loud once.

### Marks work on the boss

This is the important half. **A poisoned boss loses 25 at the start of every
round**, and it does that without you spending a card or making a check. You did
it by putting it somewhere bad.

Minions can be Marked too, and a Mark can be the thing that finishes one off.

### Shaking one off

Poison and Snared are the two that do not end on their own. **Spend one action
and both go**, together, in the same breath.

**The boss has no actions to spend, so its turn is what it spends.** A Mark you
would shake off lasts through one of its turns and is then gone. That is why a
shove into a tar pit is a good round rather than a win: it bites once.

That is the trade the module is built on: staying poisoned costs you a card
every round, and clearing it costs you a swing. Neither is obviously right, and
that is the point.

## 2. Why the timing matters

A Mark fires at the **start of the round, after Recover**.

That ordering is deliberate and it is the whole weight of a Mark. Your Spent
cards stand back up first, and then the poison takes one. So a poisoned round is
a round you fight with one card fewer than you should have had.

If Marks fired at the end of your turn instead, a card spent guarding one would
Recover before you needed it again, and Poison would cost you nothing at all.

## 3. Hazards

A **hazard** is a place. Draw one at the start of a level and put it face up
where everyone can see it, then put a brick on the table for the thing itself:
the tar pit, the frozen lake, the ember field.

> **At the start of each round, any figure standing in the hazard takes its Mark.**
>
> **Once a level, spend one action to shove the boss or a minion into it.**

There is no board and there are no squares. Where a figure is standing is
something you say out loud and everyone can see, exactly as it already is for
climbing on the boss or hiding behind a rock. Movement is still free.

| Hazard | Element | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| Tar Pit | Earth | Snared |
| Bramble Wall | Earth | Snared |
| Thorn Nest | Earth | Poison |
| Sulphur Vent | Fire | Poison |
| Ember Field | Fire | Burning |
| Frozen Lake | Water | Frozen |
| Cold Spring | Water | Frozen |
| Open Ground | Wind | Marked |

The shove is the reason a hazard is a tool and not only a threat. It costs an
action, it works once, and a boss that is Snared cannot Brace.

## 4. What the four elemental biomes do now

The Volcano, River, Mountain and Desert cards have always given an element
bonus and nothing else. With this module in play, each one also has **an object
you can use once a level, for one action**.

| Biome | Object | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Volcano | **Lava Vent** | The boss or one minion takes 50 and is Burning |
| River | **The Current** | The boss is swept off its feet and loses its next action |
| Mountain | **Rockfall** | The boss takes 75, and the scree leaves you Snared |
| Desert | **Sinkhole** | The boss is Snared, and cannot Brace while it is |

**You do not need to reprint anything for this.** A biome card has no words on
it, which is why the rule can live here instead. The card you already own is the
card this describes.

Forest, Village and Castle keep the rules they already have in
[RULES.md section 6](RULES.md) and get no object. A biome has a rule or an
object, never both.

Rockfall is the one to look at twice. It is the biggest single number in the
module and it Snares you for taking it, so it is a bet like everything else in
this game rather than a free 75.

## Quick reference

- A Mark is a brick under a figure. One thing, then it ends.
- Everything ticks at the start of the round, after Recover.
- The same Mark never stacks. Every Mark is small, and Charged is the one
  named exception.
- Poison and Snared: one action clears both.
- Hazards Mark whoever is in them, and once a level you can shove the boss in.
- Each elemental biome has one object, one action, once a level.
